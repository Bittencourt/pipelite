`<h1 className="text-3xl font-bold">{t('title')}</h1>`, so

  route            anchor key              en-US          pt-BR                   es-ES
  /organizations   organizations.title     Organizations  Empresas                Empresas
  /people          people.title            People         Pessoas                 Personas
  /deals           deals.title             Deals          Negócios                Ofertas
  /activities      activities.title        Activities     Atividades              Actividades
  /trash           trash.title             Trash          Lixeira                 Papelera
  /admin/audit     audit.retention.title   Audit Log      Registro de auditoria   Registro de auditoría

/admin/audit is the RETENTION SETTINGS page, not an audit-entry list. Do not anchor on
`audit.filter.label` or on an entry row.

Locale switching: `src/i18n/request.ts` reads exactly `cookies().get('locale')`, falling back to
`defaultLocale` = "en-US". Set the cookie with `context.addCookies([{ name: "locale", value: locale,
url: "http://localhost:3001" }])`. No UI navigation is needed.

The measured baseline (from the recorded UAT — this is the acceptance baseline, do not re-derive):
  /organizations, /people, /deals, /activities, /trash   scrollWidth 416 vs clientWidth 305
  /admin/audit                                           508 (pt-BR), 526 (es-ES) vs 305
es-ES measures WORSE than pt-BR, and that asymmetry is the exact failure mode the original Phase 36
UAT item was written to catch. es-ES is not optional.

dnd-kit activation requirements, read from @dnd-kit/core@6.3.1 and kanban-board.tsx:313-322:
  - the activator fires on React onPointerDown and requires `event.isPrimary && event.button === 0`
  - pointermove/pointerup/pointercancel listen on the OWNER DOCUMENT, so the cursor need not stay
    over the card
  - the drag starts only once `hasExceededDistance(delta, 5)` fires on a pointermove — at least one
    move must land more than 5px from the pointerdown point
  - `Resize` is wired to handleCancel: NEVER resize the viewport mid-drag
  - `Escape` is wired to handleCancel on the document

Playwright input, measured live: `page.mouse.move/down/up` emits pointerdown/pointermove/pointerup
ahead of each mouse event, every one `isTrusted: true`, `pointerType: "mouse"`, and `{ steps: 5 }`
produced 5 interpolated move pairs. This is NOT the synthetic dispatch 38-UAT refused as evidence —
that rule was about `element.dispatchEvent(new PointerEvent(...))`, which is `isTrusted: false`.
`page.keyboard.press("Escape")` is trusted for the same reason.

Do NOT use `locator.dragTo()` or `page.dragAndDrop()`: they run actionability checks on the drop
target, and `kanban-board.tsx:623` renders a `<DragOverlay>` covering the page during a drag, which
fails "receives pointer events" and hangs. Raw `page.mouse.*` performs no actionability checks.
