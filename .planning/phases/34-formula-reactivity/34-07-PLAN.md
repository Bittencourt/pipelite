<objective>
Wire the person and organization write paths to `recalculateFormulas`, always before the event emit, replicating the call pattern established in plan 34-06.

Purpose: the organization is the cascade's most important parent. It is the only entity with two child relations (deals via `deals_organization_id_idx`, people via `people_organization_id_idx`), and RESEARCH measured the worst single organization at 114 deals plus 10 people — the fan-out the D-13 budget of 500 was sized against. It is also where the D-12 fix pays off most visibly: `POST /api/v1/organizations` passes `custom_fields` straight into `createOrganizationMutation`, which discarded it entirely until plan 34-02.

Output: `createPersonMutation`, `updatePersonMutation`, `createOrganizationMutation`, `updateOrganizationMutation` and `PUT /api/v1/organizations/[id]` all recalculate before emitting, and the organization route's redundant second write is removed.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| API/UI caller → mutation `customFields` → stored JSONB | Client-supplied keys reach the database and could target a server-derived formula key |
| organization save → up to 124 child rows across two child tables | The largest fan-out surface in the dataset |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-34-04 | Tampering | caller-supplied `customFields` on people/organizations | mitigate | `stripFormulaKeys` applied before the plan 34-02 insert/merge on all four entry points, and on the `custom_fields` now routed through `updateOrganizationMutation` |
| T-34-03 | Denial of Service | organization save amplification | mitigate | The plan 34-04 budget of 500 evaluations plus depth 1 caps the worst measured case (114 deals + 10 people = 124 rows). The dotted-ref intersection gate means an organization save with no matching child formula issues zero child queries |
| T-34-19 | Tampering | organization `[id]` route double write | mitigate | The second write is REMOVED, not left as idempotent. Left in place it would land after the mutation's recalc and silently overwrite computed formula wrappers with the caller's blob — a client-controlled overwrite of server-derived data. Asserted by a source check that the route performs zero direct `db.update` calls |
| T-34-05 | Tampering | cascade to deals and people of another owner | accept | D-09: derived-value refresh is owner-independent. Only formula-typed keys are written, only on rows reachable by a real foreign key, only one hop |
| T-34-17 | Repudiation | recalc failure visibility | mitigate | Rejections are logged with a `[formula-recalc]` prefix and asserted by a test; the user's edit still succeeds per D-05 |
| T-34-SC | Tampering | npm installs | accept | Zero new packages |
</threat_model>
