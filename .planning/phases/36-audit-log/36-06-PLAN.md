<objective>
Establish the `user` actor kind at the browser-facing boundary, and give `saveFieldValues` a real
`crmBus` emit so custom-field edits — the dominant edit surface on this dataset, with 169 live
definitions — are auditable at all.

Purpose: two of the phase's five success criteria depend on this. SC-1 needs the user's name on a
browser edit; AUDIT-01's word "every" needs the custom-field write path to emit something.

**This plan contains a deliberate, user-approved behaviour change** (36-CONTEXT § Post-Research
Addendum): custom-field-only saves will fire webhooks and workflow triggers for the first time.
Existing workflows may begin reacting to saves they previously never saw. It is planned, not
incidental, and the SUMMARY must say so plainly.

Output: `runWithActor` at 15 server-action call sites and the custom-field save route; one new emit
in `src/lib/custom-fields.ts`.
</objective>
