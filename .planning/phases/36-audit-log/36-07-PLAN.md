<objective>
Feed the pre-write row into every update and delete event, so the audit subscriber has a before-value
to diff against. The pre-read already exists and is already unprojected at every one of these sites —
this costs zero extra queries and zero extra bytes.

Purpose: a subscriber fires after the write and cannot recover what a value used to be. This is
physics, not a design choice, and it is the only reason `previous` is added to a payload rather than
computed downstream.

Output: a sixth parameter on four local `buildEventPayload` helpers, forwarded at ~18 emit sites, and
the same enrichment at the three `/api/v1/{entity}/[id]` routes that emit inline.
</objective>

    event and thin on its co-emitted twin is a trap.
