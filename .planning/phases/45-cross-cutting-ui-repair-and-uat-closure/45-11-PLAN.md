    3. Wait for the app to answer on http://localhost:3001 before running anything — poll, do not

    4. `./node_modules/.bin/playwright test --project=setup` to refresh the authenticated

    5. Record the actual measured `scrollWidth` and `clientWidth` for `/admin/audit` in pt-BR and
       es-ES into the SUMMARY, beside the recorded baselines of 508 and 526. A phase that only says
       "green" has thrown away the evidence.

    If the viewport spec still fails on a route, the failure names the route and the locale and
    reports both numbers — fix the layout, rebuild, re-run. Do NOT relax the assertion, do NOT
    remove `ignoreDefaultArgs: ["--hide-scrollbars"]` (that flag is what makes the harness report the
    same 305px client width a real device does, instead of a 15px-more-permissive 320), and do NOT
    delete the anchor assertions (a blank page, an error page and a `/login` redirect all satisfy
    `scrollWidth <= clientWidth`).

    If the drag spec fails, the discriminator matters: a failure to ACTIVATE the sensor means the
    pointer sequence is wrong (check that at least one move lands more than 5px from the press and
    that `{ steps: n }` is present); a failure to LAND means collision detection needs more settle
    time; a silent cancel usually means something resized the viewport mid-drag, which dnd-kit wires
    to `handleCancel`. Never substitute `dispatchEvent` or `locator.dragTo()` — synthetic dispatch is
    refused as evidence and `dragTo` hangs on the DragOverlay.

    Do NOT add Playwright to `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. CI has no Docker, no database and no app
    server; the e2e suite is a local phase gate.
  </action>
