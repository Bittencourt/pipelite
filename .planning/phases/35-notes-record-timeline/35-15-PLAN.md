## What must NOT be removed

- Do NOT remove `notes` from the zod schemas in src/lib/mutations/{deals,organizations,people,activities}.ts

  breaking API change that belongs with the column drop, which is deferred.

- Do NOT drop the legacy column or write a migration in this plan. Dropping is a deferred idea.
</hard_constraint>
