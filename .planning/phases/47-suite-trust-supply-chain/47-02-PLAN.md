<objective>
Build the SC-1 measurement harness and record the BEFORE numbers on the unfixed tree.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC-1 was AMENDED because the original ten-run pass-set criterion **could not fail** —
16/16 identical pass sets on the unfixed tree, including at 3x oversubscription. The criterion is now
the **margin** under induced load. That margin has to be measured before anything is fixed, with a
harness that is reproducible rather than a shell loop nobody can re-run, and the harness itself needs
a floor: `IDENTICAL` over two truncated sets proves nothing, which is the exact Phase 46 lesson
(`IDENTICAL (both empty — proves nothing)`).

This plan deliberately runs on the vitest-bumped-but-otherwise-unfixed tree so that the BEFORE and
AFTER margins are measured under the same runner.

Output: `scripts/suite-repeatability.sh`, `scripts/diff-pass-sets.mjs`, and a recorded BEFORE margin
table that 47-06 will re-measure and beat.
</objective>
