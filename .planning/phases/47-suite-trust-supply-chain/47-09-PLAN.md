<objective>
Give the repository a stated, enforceable position on the advisories in its own lockfile — owned in
repo, with zero new dependencies.

Purpose: SEC-05 has two halves. 47-01, 47-07 and 47-08 discharged the *upgrade* half. This plan
discharges the *decision* half: every remaining critical and high advisory gets exactly one recorded
disposition, and the policy that says so lives in the repo as readable text with a machine-readable
allowlist beside it.

There is no acceptable off-the-shelf tool. `audit-ci` was last published **2024-07-03** and declares
`"event-stream": "4.0.1"` as a direct dependency — the package at the centre of the canonical npm
supply-chain compromise; answering a supply-chain requirement by installing 30 packages, one of them
that one, from a tool nobody has published in two years is self-defeating. `better-npm-audit` is 23
months stale on the deprecated `lodash.get`. `osv-scanner` is a Go binary, not on npm, not installed
here. Meanwhile `npm audit --json` already emits everything a policy needs, and Phase 46 landed the
exact in-repo pattern (a `.mjs` with floors, a `Fatal` class, an `import.meta.main` guard, an npm
script and a `ci.yml` step). So: **an in-repo evaluator, zero new dependencies.**

Two locked decisions this plan implements without re-litigating: the policy document lives at the
**repo root** (`AUDIT-POLICY.md`, with `audit-policy.json` beside it — a root `SECURITY.md` is a
*disclosure* policy and must not be conflated with it), and `nodemailer` 7 -> 9 takes the
**accept-with-reason-and-expiry** path rather than a breaking two-major bump.

Output: the evaluator, the allowlist, the prose policy, the npm script, and a GREEN
`npm run security:audit` on the fixed tree.
</objective>
