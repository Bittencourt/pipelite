<objective>
Make "a test asserts on elapsed time" a suite-wide failure, and delete the last two instances.

Purpose: this is ROADMAP SC-1b — the structural half of the amended criterion. 47-04 removed the
dangerous one (the 52.5-against-80 ratio). Two remain, both low risk and both strictly redundant:
`client-formula-bounds.test.ts:129` (`expect(Date.now() - start).toBeLessThan(15000)`, measured 543 ms
idle / 560 ms at 3x, and TIGHTER than its own `{ timeout: 30000 }`) and `formula-engine.test.ts:403`
(`toBeLessThan(5000)`, measured 51-59 ms). Each sits beside the assertion that actually carries the
property. The gate is what stops a third appearing.

The gate is hard for one specific reason that this phase's own research demonstrated: **version 1 of
this scanner matched only clock calls appearing inline inside `expect(...)`, and therefore missed
`condition-evaluator.test.ts:636` — the assertion QG-01 names** — because its operands were bound to
`small` and `large` first. A gate that cannot see the defect it was written for is a vacuous gate.
Taint must propagate through variable bindings to a fixpoint. Version 2, which does, was
deliberately over-broad and reported 12 raw hits of which 4 were real; the discriminating rule is
stated precisely below and must be implemented exactly.

This plan is sequenced AFTER 47-04 deliberately: that plan removed the repository's only two
`performance.now()` call sites, so this gate's clock-call token floor must be measured on the
post-deletion tree.

Output: the SC-1b gate, two deleted assertions, and negative control C's timing half observed red.
