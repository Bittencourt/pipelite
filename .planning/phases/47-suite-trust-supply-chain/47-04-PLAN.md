the replacement can fail.

elapsed measurements. At 3x oversubscription that ratio reaches **52.5 against its 80 threshold — 66%
of budget consumed by contention alone with a correct implementation**. It is one contention step from
red, and 47-CONTEXT rejects every way of hiding that (serial-only, `retries: 1`, a bigger threshold).

counter can substitute for. Both halves were prototyped and both were observed red.

this repository** — both prior git versions are `path.split(".")`. Success criterion 2's "reintroduce"
means WRITE one. Two concrete shapes are supplied.

B observed red with verbatim output.
