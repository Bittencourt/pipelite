<objective>
Sign the phase off by auditing its own evidence, not by re-asserting its intentions.

Purpose: this phase exists because v1.3 shipped **six gates that could not fail**, Phase 46's own code
review found a **seventh inside the phase built to delete them**, and Phase 47's research found this
phase's **own SC-1 could not fail** — 16/16 green on the unfixed tree. The standing assumption is
therefore that Phase 47 produced one too. The only way to find it is to read every recorded control and
ask whether its output is real.

46-06 established the shape and the standard: a per-control table with the decisive line quoted, a
one-line verdict per ROADMAP criterion, and two gaps reported as NOT PROVED rather than inferred. That
is the bar. A gate with no recorded red observation is treated as **not delivered**, and saying so is
more useful than a table of ticks.

This plan changes no source file.

Output: the negative-control register, the success-criterion verdicts, the final gate sweep, and an
honest list of anything unproven.
