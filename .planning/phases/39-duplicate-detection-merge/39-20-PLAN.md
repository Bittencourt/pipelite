`compareChanges` sorts by `group`, then `rank`, then `label.localeCompare`, then `field`.

**WHY APPENDING IS ORDER-NEUTRAL, AND THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING ARGUMENT OF THE PLAN.** Today

mapped column and to every custom field is therefore IDENTICAL. The one ordering that does change:
against another *unmapped* native column in the same entry, `notes` now sorts BEFORE it instead of
being tie-broken by `label.localeCompare` — today the only such column is `deletedAt`, which
`audit-entry.tsx` intercepts and renders as a translated sentence with no value cell. Record this in
the summary; do not present the change as "no ordering change at all".
